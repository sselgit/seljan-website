DNS report

Is this domain set up well?

We gather the important records and check them against common good practice, then list problems first and explain each one.

Looks things up straight from your browser. When you press the button, this page asks Google Public DNS or Cloudflare, whichever you choose directly, with no cookies and no referrer. We never see the lookup. Private addresses and internal names are never sent. Nothing is saved, and reloading clears everything.

This makes about 15 DNS queries. Findings are guidance, not a certification, and some are judgement calls.
