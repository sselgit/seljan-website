Timestamps

What time was that, really?

Paste a timestamp from a log, a database or a file. We show every plausible reading, in UTC and in the zone you choose.

Runs only in your browser. What you paste or load here is never uploaded, saved, or sent anywhere. This page cannot make network requests at all, and it uses no cookies or storage. Reloading or closing the tab clears everything.

Numbers are read as Unix seconds, milliseconds, microseconds or nanoseconds, Windows FILETIME, Chrome/WebKit time, .NET ticks, Apple Cocoa time and Excel serials. Years before 1996 or after 2100 are marked unlikely.
